## Totals

- Assets to download: 64 models + 20 texture sets = 275 MB, plus about 42 MB of npm tools.
- Estimated added to the game: about 20-30 MB on desktop, 8-12 MB on phones (to be measured and reported). Interiors load when you reach them.

## Not found as CC0, so made in code instead

- Realistic cars, the truck and bikes: no CC0 models that look real (the free CC0 car packs are cartoon style; realistic CC0 cars on Sketchfab need an account). Cars and the truck are rebuilt with proper curved bodies, glass, chrome and tyres; bikes are built in code. The Poly Haven covered car is used where cars sit under covers.
- Trees, hedges and grass: the Poly Haven scanned trees are millions of triangles (the smallest usable one is 214 MB). Trees are grown in the browser with ez-tree using the CC0 bark and leaf textures; hedges and grass are generated; all of them sway in the wind.
- Posters and graffiti: original designs drawn in code, invented names only.
