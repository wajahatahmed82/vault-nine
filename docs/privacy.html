VAULT NINE

Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

Vault Nine collects no personal data. Nothing you do in the game is sent anywhere. Your settings and progress stay on your own device.

What the game stores

To remember your settings (volume, sensitivity, graphics quality) and your progress (level, unlocked weapons, best scores), the game saves a small amount of data in your device's local storage.

  • This data never leaves your device.
  • It contains nothing that identifies you: no name, email address, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site data in your browser, deletes it completely.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No sharing or selling of data with anyone, because no data is collected.
  • No network requests during play. The game runs entirely on your device.

Permissions

The Android app includes the standard internet permission that the app framework requires. The game itself does not use it to send or receive any data.

Children

Vault Nine is a shooter intended for players aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game ever changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the game's public issue tracker: github.com/wajahatahmed82/vault-nine/issues.

Vault Nine is an original game. All names, characters and locations are fictional.
