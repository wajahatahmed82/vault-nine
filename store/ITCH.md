# Vault Nine on itch.io (free, about 15 minutes)

The fastest way to get real players. itch.io runs browser games as they are, and it works on desktop and phone browsers.

1. Create an account at https://itch.io and choose **Upload new project**.
2. **Title:** Vault Nine. **Kind of project:** HTML.
3. **Uploads:** add `vault-nine-itch.zip` from this folder and tick **This file will be played in the browser**.
4. **Embed options:**
   - Viewport: 1280 × 720
   - Tick **Mobile friendly**, orientation **Landscape**
   - Tick **Fullscreen button**
5. **Pricing:** free, or "name your own price" if you want to accept tips.
6. **Description, screenshots, cover image:** use `LISTING.md`, the screenshots, and `feature-1024x500.jpg` cropped to 630 × 500 (or the icon).
7. **Genre:** Shooter. **Tags:** fps, singleplayer, story-rich, stealth, three-js.
8. Save as a draft, open the page and play it once, then set it to **Public**.

When the game changes, ask me for a new zip and upload it over the old one; the page URL stays the same.
